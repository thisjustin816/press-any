CRT 240p, CRT 480p and Scanlines are this app's own Metal shader, modelled on
CRT-Lottes: a Gaussian beam across each line, a Gaussian reconstruction along it,
light in linear sRGB and a phosphor mask, with CRT-Lottes's default beam hardness.
The device color profiles are ported with the upstream sRGB matrices and default
luminance, without additional darkening.
