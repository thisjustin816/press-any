The app ports these algorithms to Metal. CRT-Lottes retains the upstream default
Gaussian reconstruction, beam, bloom, mask and sRGB transfer settings. The app
adds raster-edge feathering, edge falloff, frame blending and a woven-field
adapter for its Game Boy Player presentation. Device color profiles use the
upstream sRGB matrices and default luminance without additional darkening.
