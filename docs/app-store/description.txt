Press Any is a Game Boy and Game Boy Color emulator for iPhone, built for players and people making Game Boy games.

Keep every version of a game together. Import a ROM, review its details, and organize original releases, homebrew revisions, and patched versions in one library. Press Any can find a recognized game's artwork and ask you to review it.

Choose how games look and feel. Nostalgic, the default, is the game as you remember it, polished for today's screens. Accurate is as the original looked and felt, from its own screen to the television a Super Game Boy or Game Boy Player played on. Clean is just the game, with no effects added. All three are free, and the Game Boy palettes stay free.

Automatic picks the right hardware for each game: Game Boy Color games on a Game Boy Color, Super Game Boy games on a Super Game Boy, and games with Game Boy Advance extras on a Game Boy Advance.

Keep your progress organized with Save Profiles. Use separate saves for different playthroughs, share compatible saves between versions, and return to an exact moment with save states.

Want to try something quickly? Quick Play opens a ROM without adding it to your library. Add it later and keep your progress.

Features:
• Game Boy and Game Boy Color emulation powered by SameBoy
• Game Boy Printer: prints come out of a drawn printer, ready to save or share
• Game Boy Camera through your iPhone's camera, and tilt cartridges that follow your iPhone
• Touch controls and compatible game controller support
• Save Profiles and save states
• IPS and BPS patching while preserving your original files
• Fast Forward
• Import review, duplicate detection and artwork lookup
• Technical information and homebrew toolchain detection
• Offline play with your library stored on your device

Press Any Plus, a one-time purchase, adds choosing the hardware each game runs on, changing each gameplay setting on its own, app icon colors, Auto State history and Timed States, and more print backgrounds and history.

No games are included. Import your own .gb and .gbc files to play.

Requires iOS 17.4 or later.
