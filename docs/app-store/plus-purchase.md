The values to enter in App Store Connect (Apps > Press Any > Monetization > In-App Purchases).
How to set up agreements, the Small Business Program and the first review is in
[TestFlight](../testflight.md#9-set-up-press-any-plus); what Plus includes is in the Pricing section of
[product.md](../product.md).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Non-Consumable |
| Reference Name | Press Any Plus |

## Review information

A screenshot of Settings > Press Any Plus from an iPhone, and these notes:

> Settings > Press Any Plus shows what Plus includes, the price, Buy and Restore Purchases. Plus
> unlocks changing the Gameplay preset's settings one by one (Settings > Game Boy > Customize,
> also in a game's settings; the Nostalgic, Accurate and Clean presets and the screen palettes
> are free), app icon colors (Settings > App Icon), Auto State history and Timed States
> (Settings > Playing > Keep Auto States and Timed States), Model, which runs a game on other
> Game Boy hardware (Settings > Playing > Model), and, for Game Boy Printer games, the Wood,
> Printer, Game Artwork and Custom print backgrounds in the print preview and a longer print
> history (Settings > Playing > Keep Prints). Everything else is free and works without it.
