Game Boy Printer
Games that print now work. The slip comes out of a drawn printer, and you can save or share it as a PNG with a transparent, light or dark background. The Wood, Printer, Game Artwork and Custom backgrounds and a longer print history are part of Plus. Prints are kept with each Game in Captures, and a Printer Log helps when a game does not print.

Game Boy Camera
Camera cartridges see through your iPhone's camera, with color capture on compatible ROMs. Choose the rear camera, the front camera or none in Settings or the game menu.

Tilt games
Cartridges with a motion sensor follow how you tilt your iPhone. Recenter Tilt is in the game menu.

Model (Plus)
Run games on a Game Boy, Game Boy Pocket, Super Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance or Game Boy Player, each with its own boot ROM.

Artwork
After you import a recognized game, Press Any can find its artwork and ask you to review what it found. Turn this off in Settings.
